

# Business Insight 360 — Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

**Business Insight 360** is an interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence dashboard** designed to provide a 360° view of business performance across **Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive Management**.

The project brings multiple business perspectives into a single analytical solution, allowing users to move from high-level KPIs to detailed analysis of products, customers, markets, regions, and forecasting performance.

**Tools:** Microsoft Power BI • Power Query • DAX • Data Modeling • Data Visualization

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The dashboard is designed to help business stakeholders:

- Monitor overall **Net Sales, Gross Margin, and Net Profit**.
- Understand **sales and profitability trends** over time.
- Compare actual performance with **Last Year (LY) and Benchmark (BM)**.
- Identify high- and low-performing **products, customers, markets, and regions**.
- Evaluate **market share and revenue contribution**.
- Measure **forecast accuracy and forecasting errors**.
- Provide executives with a consolidated view for faster, data-driven decision-making.

# 📈 Important Business Insights

### 1. Strong Sales Scale

The dashboard reports **$43.54B in Net Sales**, with the displayed benchmark comparison showing **80.12%**.

### 2. Gross Margin Needs Attention

Gross Margin is **36.97%**, compared with a benchmark of **37.41%**, showing a **1.18% negative variance**.

### 3. Profitability Is the Major Concern

Despite the strong sales scale, **Net Profit % is -17.49%**, indicating that operating expenses and other costs are significantly affecting final profitability.

### 4. Forecast Accuracy Has Improved

Forecast Accuracy is **84.38%**, compared with **79.65% LY**, representing a **5.95% improvement** shown by the dashboard.

### 5. Forecast Error Has Increased

Absolute Error is **$168M**, compared with **$130.54M LY**, representing a **28.47% increase**.

This indicates that while the accuracy KPI has improved, the business still has substantial forecasting error in absolute dollar terms.

### 6. Customer Concentration

The displayed Top 5 customers account for **39.3% of revenue contribution**, with Amazon contributing **12.8%** among the listed customers.

### 7. Product Profitability Variation

Products show different Gross Margin percentages and GM% variances. For example, the displayed products range from approximately **33.77% to 36.27% GM** among the leading products shown.
